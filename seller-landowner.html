SELLER & LANDOWNER CENTRE

Prepare before you sell or develop

A clear brief and organised documents help make the first conversation more useful. Avoid sharing original documents or sensitive identity data through unsecured channels.

1. Request a valuation review

Record property type, location, extent and the purpose of valuation. Verified evidence is required for a defensible opinion.

2. Prepare your documents

Review title chain, EC, tax receipts, survey details, approvals and co-owner authority as relevant.

3. Explore a joint venture

Compare assumed area, price, cost and sharing scenarios before negotiations.

Run JV scenario →

Questions to prepare

  • What is your preferred outcome: outright sale, rental income or joint venture?
  • Who are the legal owners, and are there mortgages, disputes or restrictions?
  • What are the exact survey number, extent, road access and property boundaries?
  • What is your expected timeline and minimum acceptable commercial outcome?
  • Have an independent advocate and architect reviewed title and development potential?
