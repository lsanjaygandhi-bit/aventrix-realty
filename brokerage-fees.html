Our Approach

Every Property Assignment Is Different

A residential purchase, a land transaction, a commercial lease and a Joint Venture assignment can each involve very different levels of work, coordination and responsibility.

That is why Aventrix Realty does not apply one fixed fee to every assignment. We first understand your requirement, define the scope of our involvement, and then agree the applicable professional fee, commonly referred to as brokerage, with you.

The Process

How We Agree Your Fee

The same four steps apply to every assignment, whatever its size.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We understand your property, requirement and objectives.

  2. 02

    Define

    We establish the scope of our involvement and the services required.

  3. 03

    Agree

    The applicable professional fee, payment terms and applicable taxes are discussed and confirmed.

  4. 04

    Proceed

    We begin the assignment once the terms are mutually agreed.

What You Can Expect

Our Commitment

Clear Communication

You will know the applicable fee before we proceed.

Written Terms

The agreed fee and payment terms are clearly documented where applicable.

No Unexpected Changes

The agreed fee does not change unless the scope changes and revised terms are mutually agreed.

Professional Transparency

We explain what the fee covers, which party it applies to and when it is payable.

Taxes & Invoicing

Where applicable, GST and other statutory taxes will be charged in accordance with applicable laws. Applicable fees and taxes will be communicated as part of the agreed commercial terms.
