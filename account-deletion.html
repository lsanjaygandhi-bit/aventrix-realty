Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your Aventrix Realty account and personal data

This page explains how users of the Aventrix Realty Android app (and the website) can ask us to delete their account and the personal data linked to it. You do not need to be signed in to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below using your full name and the email address you registered with in the app.
  2. Tick the confirmation box and submit the request.
  3. Our team will verify the request using your registered email address and confirm with you once your account and data have been deleted.

You can also email us at office@aventrixrealty.com from your registered email address, or call +91 91768 87770.

What will be deleted

Deleted on request

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address and password)
  • Your profile details (name, phone number, preferred contact method)
  • Your saved items: wishlist, shortlist and recently viewed properties
  • Your saved preferences and notifications
  • Information you previously submitted, where you ask us to remove it

May be retained

  • Enquiry records and client communications, kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain records of those communications (see our Privacy Policy)
  • Records we are required to keep to comply with applicable law, or to protect the rights of Aventrix Realty, our clients or others
  • Anonymous property-view statistics that do not include your name, phone number or email address

Once an account is deleted it cannot be recovered. Data that is retained is kept only for the purposes above, and we will tell you in our confirmation if anything specific has been kept and why. Retention is governed by the Privacy Policy.
