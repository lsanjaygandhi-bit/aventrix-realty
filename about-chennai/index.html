MASTER CHRONOLOGY

Chennai through the centuries

The chronology connects verified city-wide milestones to the locality studies below. Each area follows its own timeline.

Before 1639

Settlements before modern Madras

The Chennai region had settlements and cultural landscapes long before the English East India Company established its foothold in 1639. The exact history varies by locality and should be read through archaeological evidence, inscriptions, village records and dated maps; 1639 marks a colonial-era milestone, not the beginning of settlement.

Research framework
1639–1700

Early Madras and Fort St. George

The Madraspatnam grant of 1639 and the establishment of Fort St. George in 1640 are central milestones in the formation of English Madras. The early town grew alongside existing settlements and communities; the Fort’s chronology should not be treated as a complete history of the wider region.

Official historical source identified
1700–1800

Trade, governance and urban formation

The town’s growth was shaped by trade, craft production, colonial administration and conflict. The surviving built-up area and street pattern reflect several phases of change; street-level claims need to be tied to dated maps, gazetteers or archival records.

Research framework
1800–1947

Railways, port, institutions and industry

Rail, port activity, public institutions and industry altered how Madras connected to its hinterland and how its residential districts grew. Exact opening dates and locality-level effects should be cited individually rather than inferred from the broad period.

Milestones require individual citations
1947–1980

Post-independence urban expansion

After Independence, industrial and institutional growth, suburban rail, planned housing and changes in local administration contributed to outward urban expansion. The sequence differed across the southern suburbs, western industrial areas and established city neighbourhoods; individual layout histories remain parcel-specific.

Research framework
1980–2010

Suburbanisation, automobiles and IT

From the late twentieth century, road investment, industrial estates, technology employment and new housing reshaped several metropolitan corridors. The timing and scale of change differed by segment, and a transport or employment milestone alone does not prove a particular property-price increase.

Research framework
2010 onwards

Metropolitan transformation

Recent metropolitan change includes new transit infrastructure, road projects, employment centres and housing extending into adjoining districts. Planning boundaries, local-body jurisdictions and environmental constraints have changed over time and must be checked against the relevant dated government documents.

Planning documents identified

LAND PRICE HISTORY

Understanding land prices

Three different measures help explain land values. They must be compared separately, not treated as the same price.

A

Government guideline value

The value notified or displayed for registration purposes for the relevant street, village or survey reference and effective period.

  • Source: Tamil Nadu Registration Department / official records
  • Record the exact street or survey number, unit and effective date
  • Do not infer a historical value from a current search result
B

Registered transaction price

Evidence recorded in registered sale deeds and related instruments, where the underlying document can be accessed and lawfully used.

  • Record date, property identifiers, extent and stated consideration
  • Separate land-only sales from built-up property transactions
  • State the sample size and limitations
C

Practical market price

Evidence about prices buyers and sellers were negotiating or achieving, supported by dated market studies, comparable transactions or reliable historical records.

  • Mark asking prices separately from completed deals
  • Compare similar land types and locations
  • Do not describe anecdotal quotations as an area-wide average

Historical property prices: verified records only

Historic price figures are published only when the locality, street or survey reference, applicable period, unit and source can be verified. Guideline values, registered sale consideration and advertised prices are shown separately. The research register will record unavailable data rather than fill gaps with guesses.

Verified locality research and development milestones

Chennai’s urban form grew in layers: older temple and coastal settlements; the fortified trade town around Fort St George; weaving and merchant settlements; colonial roads, institutions and garden houses; planned neighbourhoods; railway-linked suburbs; industrial estates; and more recent IT and coastal corridors. A neighbourhood’s current property identity is the result of several periods, not a single launch date.

Planned T. Nagar / Mambalam

T. Nagar’s planned formation is commonly associated with the Mambalam Town Planning Scheme under the Raja of Panagal government (1921–1925), including Panagal Park and radial streets. Historian Sriram V.’s archival research reports a 1924 planning file. T. Nagar later shifted from primarily residential streets toward a major retail district from the mid-20th century. The scheme is a documented planning milestone; each later subdivision still needs its own plan and deed.

Read the archival planning account · Background chronology (secondary)

Poes Garden

A historical account traces the name to a Mr Poe and an 1837 map. The same account describes the estate later becoming Binny’s property and being divided into residential plots in the 1950s. This establishes an estate-to-plots sequence, but the original layout approval and exact promoter record should be sought before assigning a formal layout name or number.

Madras Musings — Poes Garden history

Boat Club / R. A. Puram

A 2018 heritage-walk report describes 18th-century land purchases associated with George Mowbray and John DeMonte. These are important leads into the area’s estate history, but secondary reporting must be checked against deeds, wills and later conveyances before treating it as a full parcel history.

New Indian Express heritage-walk report

Chromepet and the southern suburban belt

Madras Institute of Technology was founded in July 1949 by C. Rajam and became part of Anna University in 1978. This provides a firm institutional milestone for Chromepet’s education-led context. The exact promoter and year of any nearby layout must still come from its approved plan and deeds.

MIT official history

OMR and Sholinganallur

TIDEL Park opened in 2000; Tamil Nadu IT Department records the Sholinganallur SEZ extent and company allotments, while TNRDC describes the 45 km Rajiv Gandhi Salai corridor. These provide a documented employment and infrastructure timeline, not a single date for all private housing growth.

Land-price evidence: the three series must remain separate

SeriesDefinitionResearch method
Government guideline valueAdministrative benchmark used for registration purposes; it can change by effective date and street / village classification.TNREGINET offers historical guideline lookup from 2002. Record the exact street, revenue village, SRO, classification, unit and effective period.
Registered transaction considerationPrice stated in a registered deed for a particular property and date.Use deed / EC-level records with parcel identity, extent, property type and date. One transaction is not a locality-wide average.
Market / asking priceCurrent advertised price or a properly constructed market estimate.Capture listing date, exact location, land/building distinction and whether it is asking or completed-sale evidence.
Worked example — Boat Club Road guideline value only: A secondary data mirror attributed to TNREGINET reports ₹23,000/sq.ft. from 1 April 2023 and ₹25,300/sq.ft. from 1 July 2024. Treat this as a cross-check lead, not a completed sale or current market price; verify the exact Mylapore Part 1/Part 2 street classification in the official registration portal. Older series on the mirror contain gaps, so they are not represented as a continuous historical trend.

Records needed to name a layout promoter accurately

  • Approved layout plan and approval number / date from CMDA or the relevant local planning authority.
  • Original sale deed, parent deed and subsequent conveyances naming the landowner/promoter.
  • FMB sketch, village map, survey number and subdivision record.
  • Government Order or acquisition / assignment file where public land or a public agency was involved.
  • For historic prices: dated registered deeds or official guideline schedules, with units and exact geography preserved.
