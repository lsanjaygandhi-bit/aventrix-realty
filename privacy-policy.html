Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Aventrix Realty ("we", "us", "our") operates the website aventrixrealty.com and the Aventrix Realty mobile application (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Services, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

By using our website or mobile app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to provide it to us, primarily through enquiry and contact forms on our website and within the mobile app (for example, the property enquiry form, free valuation form, joint venture form, and the "Enquire" feature in the mobile app). This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (where provided)
  • Any message or property requirement details you choose to share with us

We do not currently require you to create an account or log in to use the website or mobile app, and we do not collect passwords or account credentials from visitors browsing properties, using search or filters, or viewing our team and office information.

The mobile app's Shortlist feature stores the properties you save locally on your own device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Call, WhatsApp and Map Links

Our website and mobile app include direct "Call", "WhatsApp" and "Map" links to help you reach our team or locate our offices. When you tap or click these links, your device's own phone, WhatsApp, or maps application opens to complete that action. Any information you share through a phone call or WhatsApp conversation initiated this way is governed by the privacy practices of your phone provider and of WhatsApp/Google Maps respectively, not by Aventrix Realty directly, though we will handle any information you share with us during that conversation in line with this policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to us solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, valuation request, or message
  • Provide you with information about properties, services, or opportunities you have asked about
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding your enquiry
  • Improve our website and mobile app based on how they are used

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Information you submit through an enquiry form is used internally by the Aventrix Realty team to respond to you.

We may share information where required to do so by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Aventrix Realty, our clients, or others.

Our website and mobile app are built using Supabase for backend data storage. Supabase acts as our data processor and stores enquiry information on our behalf, under its own security and hosting practices.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and to maintain records of client communications, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website and mobile app may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube. These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

Your Rights and Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information you have previously submitted to us

To make any of these requests, please contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
