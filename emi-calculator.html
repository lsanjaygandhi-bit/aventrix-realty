Tips

  1. A longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
  2. A higher down payment reduces the loan principal, which lowers both the EMI and the total interest.
  3. A lower interest rate reduces the total interest payable, so it helps to compare offers from more than one lender.
  4. Where your lender allows part-prepayment, paying down principal early reduces the outstanding balance and can shorten the tenure.
  5. On a floating-rate loan, a change in the rate can change your EMI or your remaining tenure.
