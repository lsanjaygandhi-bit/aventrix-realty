Know your number

Three different “rates” — and why they differ

Government guideline value
The minimum value notified by the Registration Department for stamp duty and registration, searchable on TNREGINET by village / street and survey number. It is a statutory reference, not a market price, and can sit well above or below what buyers actually pay.
Online asking price
What sellers are asking on listing portals. Useful for seeing supply, but asking prices are often negotiated down and include unsold or overpriced stock.
Registered transaction price
The consideration stated in registered sale deeds. The closest evidence of actual prices, but it needs comparable properties, recent dates and a check that the stated value reflects the real deal.

An indicative figure from this page is not a valuation report and must not be used for lending, legal, tax or court purposes. Plot shape, frontage, road width, approvals, title, floor, age and condition can move the value of a specific property well outside a locality rate.
