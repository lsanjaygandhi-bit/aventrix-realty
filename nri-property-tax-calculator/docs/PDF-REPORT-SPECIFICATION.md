# PDF Report Specification

## Branding
- Use the original Aventrix Realty logo supplied by the owner without altering its artwork.
- Brand palette: black, green and white.
- Keep typography restrained, tables readable, ample spacing and clear page numbers.
- Use a small logo/header on subsequent pages; avoid stretching the logo.

## Required sections
1. Cover and report metadata
2. Scope and status (preliminary estimate / documents reconciled / professional review completed)
3. Tax profile and property ownership
4. Purchase/acquisition chronology
5. Proposed sale details
6. Document and evidence checklist
7. Calculation walkthrough with formulas and amounts
8. Capital gains analysis and legal classification (only if validated)
9. TDS/withholding scenarios (only if validated)
10. Exemptions and reinvestment questions
11. Foreign tax / treaty / repatriation review checklist
12. Missing information, assumptions and exclusions
13. Professional reconciliation checklist
14. Sources, rule version, generated timestamp and disclaimer

## Suggested disclaimer
“This report has been prepared by Aventrix Realty for preliminary reference and informational purposes based on information supplied by the user. It is not a final tax computation, tax opinion, or confirmation of tax liability. Please share this report and supporting documents with your qualified Chartered Accountant or relevant tax professional for independent verification, reconciliation with original records, and confirmation of the applicable tax treatment. Final liability and withholding obligations depend on the facts, supporting evidence and law applicable to the transaction.”

## PDF generation controls
- Generate from a stable report snapshot so printed figures match the screen.
- Never show an unreviewed calculation as certified or final.
- Include report ID and rule version; exclude unnecessary identifiers.
- Avoid permanent server-side PDF storage by default.
